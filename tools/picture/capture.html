Result: what the game gets

Looks:

Paper

Makes the paper white all over, even with a shadow or yellow light on it.
Higher also removes pale colours and pencil marks.
White left inside the outline, such as the eyes, stays white instead of see-through.
Dust, crumbs and stray dots around the drawing.

Colour

Brings out crayon colours that came out pale.
Turns every colour round the rainbow.
Evens out the scribbles inside each colour.

Style

Fewer colours give flat patches, like a cartoon.
Big pixels, like an old console game.
Draws a line round the whole drawing, so it stands out on any scenery.
