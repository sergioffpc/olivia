# Drawing sheets

*Translated from `folhas_para_desenhar.pdf`. Each label is where the drawing goes under `assets/`.*

| Sheet | Instruction | Label | Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| The hero | Draw each part inside its box, from the front. Connect it to the black dot. | `hero` | `hero/*.png` |
| Who is saved | Draw each part inside its box, from the front. Connect it to the black dot. | `princess` | `princess/*.png` |
| The hero's launcher | Draw what the hero uses to fire things. The hand holds it at the black dot. | `hero/weapon` | `hero/launcher.png` |
| What the hero fires | Draw the thing the hero fires. Fill the whole box. | `fx/projectile` | `fx/projectile.png` |
| The fart cloud | Draw the cloud that comes out when the hero does the extra jump. Fill the whole box. | `fx/fart` | `fx/fart.png` |
| A baddie that crawls | Draw a long, low baddie that crawls along. Fill the whole box. | `enemy_crawl` | `enemy_crawl.png` |
| A baddie that rolls | Draw a round baddie that will spin as it rolls. Fill the whole box. | `enemy_roll` | `enemy_roll.png` |
| A baddie that floats | Draw a baddie that floats through the air. Fill the whole box. | `enemy_float` | `enemy_float.png` |
| The scenery | Draw what is seen far away, behind the hero. Don't draw the ground. | `level/background` | `level/background.png` |
| The ground | Draw the ground the hero walks and jumps on. Colour the whole strip, end to end. | `level/platform` | `level/platform.png` |
| What gets picked up | Draw the thing the hero picks up along the way. Fill the whole box. | `level/coin` | `level/coin.png` |
| Where they are held | Draw the place where the one to be saved is held. Only the place, without the character. | `level/goal` | `level/goal.png` |
| The numbers | Write and decorate the number of each box, nice and big, inside the box! | `hud/digit_0..9` | `hud/digit_0.png` to `hud/digit_9.png` |
| The lives | Draw the symbol of the hero's lives. | `hud/life`, `hud/life_lost` | `hud/life.png`, `hud/life_lost.png` |
